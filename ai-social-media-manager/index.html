Will it actually sound like me?

It’s the right first question to ask about any AI content. Generic posts are easy to spot. LiveLoop is built so your posts read like you wrote them, and look like you, because they start with you.

Want it to literally sound like you? Signature Voice Notes are short notes in your own AI voice, made only with your consent.

  • Your voice comes first

    Before it writes a word, LiveLoop learns your story, your voice and your topics through your Signature DNA™, kept in your Brand Kit. Every post is written from it.

  • Your real photos, not stock

    Your posts use branded images made from your own headshots. Your face and your look, every week.

  • You approve your week

    Nothing posts until you say so. Every caption gets an editor check first. Review the week’s drafts, then approve the whole week in one tap, edit a post, or pass on it. If something still needs your OK, you get a heads-up before it’s due.

  • It learns what works for you

    Publish. Learn. Repeat. LiveLoop learns over time from the posts you pick and how they perform.

Done-for-you social media content, in four steps.

  1. Share your story

    A Signature DNA interview captures your story, your voice and your topics. It becomes your Brand Kit.

  2. Bring your photos

    Your headshots become branded images, so every post shows the real you.

  3. Approve your week

    Each week LiveLoop plans and writes your posts. You review them and approve the week in one tap.

  4. It posts on schedule

    Posts go out to your Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn. Each month becomes your Monthly Issue, a digital magazine of your month.
