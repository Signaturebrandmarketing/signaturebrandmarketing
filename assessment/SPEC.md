# AI Readiness Assessment — spec v1
*Ezra, 2026-09-18. Ken's gate: approve the ten questions + the result copy (15 min). Lives at signaturebrandmarketing.com/assessment. Insurance and tow "skins" later = same engine, different examples.*

## What it does
1. Ten questions, one screen each, four choices. Two minutes. No login, no email wall before the questions.
2. Score 0–100 shown on screen immediately, with Brand / Growth / Automation sub-scores and the three biggest gaps — each gap named plainly, with the fix (what Signature does about it).
3. Contact step *after* the score is shown (demo-before-ask): name · email · business · phone + text-consent checkbox. Submit → full report emailed, contact lands in GHL with score/tier/gaps/industry as custom fields + tag `ai-readiness`, Ken notified.
4. One CTA everywhere: **Book a 15-minute readiness call.**

- Team: Just me · 2–5 · 6–15 · 16+
- Annual revenue: Under $100K · $100K–$250K · $250K–$1M · $1M+

## The ten questions (each answer scores 0 / 3 / 7 / 10)

**BRAND**
1. **If a stranger looked you up online tonight, what would they find?**
   a. Not much — a social profile, maybe a listing *(0)*
   b. A website or page, but it's old and doesn't sound like me *(3)*
   c. A current site, but it doesn't say what makes me different *(7)*
   d. A site, imagery, and a message that sound exactly like me *(10)*
2. **How does content go out under your name?**
   a. It doesn't, or only when I remember *(0)*
   b. A few times a month, when I have time *(3)*
   c. Weekly — but I write and post all of it myself *(7)*
   d. On a schedule, produced and published for me *(10)*
3. **Do you have a written positioning — who you serve, what you do, why you?**
   a. No *(0)*
   b. It's in my head *(3)*
   c. Written, but nobody uses it *(7)*
   d. Written, and it's on everything we put out *(10)*

**GROWTH**
4. **Where do new clients come from today?**
   a. Referrals and word of mouth only *(0)*
   b. Referrals plus some social media *(3)*
   c. Referrals plus one lead source I control *(7)*
   d. Several sources I control, and I know the numbers *(10)*
5. **When a new lead comes in, what happens in the first hour?**
   a. Nothing until I see it *(0)*
   b. I reach out when I can — sometimes that's tomorrow *(3)*
   c. Someone on my team follows up within the day *(7)*
   d. A response goes out automatically, right away *(10)*
6. **Can you see every lead and exactly where it stands, on one screen?**
   a. No — it's in my head and my phone *(0)*
   b. A spreadsheet or notes *(3)*
   c. A CRM I don't really keep up *(7)*
   d. A CRM that's current and I trust it *(10)*

**AUTOMATION**
7. **How much of your follow-up depends on somebody remembering?**
   a. All of it *(0)*
   b. Most of it *(3)*
   c. Some — a few things are automated *(7)*
   d. Almost none — the system fires and stops when they reply *(10)*
8. **A message comes in at 9 PM. What happens?**
   a. It waits until morning *(0)*
   b. I answer it if I happen to see it *(3)*
   c. My assistant handles it the next day *(7)*
   d. It's answered and booked automatically *(10)*
9. **What happens to the calls you can't pick up?**
   a. Voicemail — I call back when I can *(0)*
   b. Voicemail — and most of them never call back *(3)*
   c. An answering service takes a message *(7)*
   d. They're answered and booked, day or night *(10)*
10. **If you took two weeks off, would leads still get answered, followed up, and booked?**
   a. No *(0)*
   b. Some would *(3)*
   c. Most would *(7)*
   d. Yes — it runs without me *(10)*

## Scoring
- Total = sum of ten answers (0–100). Pillars: Brand = Q1–3 (of 30) · Growth = Q4–6 (of 30) · Automation = Q7–10 (of 40). Shown as percentages.
- **Tiers**
  - 0–35 — **Running on memory.** *"Your business works because you do. Every lead, every follow-up, every post runs through you — which means it stops when you do."*
  - 36–65 — **Pieces, not a system.** *"You've got real parts — a site, some content, maybe a CRM — but they don't hand off to each other. The gaps between them are where the money leaks."*
  - 66–85 — **Almost automated.** *"The foundation's solid. A few pieces still depend on somebody remembering. Close those and it runs."*
  - 86–100 — **Ready to scale.** *"You're running a system. The question now is capacity — more leads in, and a team on one screen."*
- **Top 3 gaps** = the three lowest-scoring questions (ties → lower question number first). Each maps to a fix line:

| Q | Gap (plain) | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Nobody can find you — or what they find doesn't sound like you | Brand kit + a site that reads like you |
| 2 | Content only happens when you have time | A content engine that produces and publishes for you |
| 3 | No written positioning | Positioning session: who, what, why — on everything |
| 4 | Growth depends on referrals | A lead source you control — funnels + outbound |
| 5 | Leads wait for you | Instant response the second a lead lands |
| 6 | No single view of the pipeline | A branded CRM with every lead on one board |
| 7 | Follow-up runs on memory | Sequences that fire on their own, stop when they reply |
| 8 | After-hours messages wait | An inbox agent that answers and books, day or night |
| 9 | Missed calls are lost calls | AI answering that books the job or the appointment |
| 10 | The business can't run without you | The whole system — brand, growth, automation — installed |
