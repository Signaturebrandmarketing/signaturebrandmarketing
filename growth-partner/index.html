Questions

The ones everybody asks.

How is this different from Signature Funnels?

Funnels fixes what's leaking. Growth Partner builds what's missing. Funnels is a system you install — the same proven build for everybody, live in about a week, using whatever brand you already have. Growth Partner is a team you hire to build the brand itself and create demand that doesn't exist yet. If the phone rings and you're losing people, buy Funnels. If it doesn't ring enough, Funnels has nothing to catch.

Is the automation included, or do I buy both?

Included. Everything Signature Funnels does is part of Growth Partner — the missed-call text-back, the AI chat, the follow-up, the CRM. You never buy both.

How long before I see something?

You'll see positioning work inside the first two weeks and brand work inside the first phase. But be clear-eyed: this is a build, not a seven-day install. The businesses this works for are the ones that wanted a foundation, not a launch.

Do I have to be a certain size?

It's less about size than about whether there's something to build on — revenue coming in, a service people already pay for, and the capacity to handle more work when it shows up. If more leads would break you right now, we'll tell you to fix that first.

What if I already have a brand I like?

Then we don't rebuild it. Phase one becomes positioning and messaging, and everything moves faster. We're not interested in redoing work that's already right.

Why by application?

Because a team actually runs these, so we take a small number at a time. The call is also how we tell you it isn't a fit — and when that's the answer, we'll point you at the thing that is.
