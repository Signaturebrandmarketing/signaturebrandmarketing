What should an insurance agent post?

People buy insurance from someone they know and trust. Your page is where they get to know you, long before they need a quote. The hard part is showing up every week while you’re in the field.

Here’s the mix LiveLoop plans for agents, built from your Signature DNA.

  • Coverage, explained simply

    Plain-language posts on what a policy does, so people understand their options before they call you.

    “Liability limits, explained in 60 seconds.”

  • Life moments

    A new home, a new baby, a teen behind the wheel, retirement. The moments when people realize they need an agent.

    “Just bought your first home? Three questions to ask your agent.”

  • Seasonal reminders

    Timely posts that give people a reason to review their coverage, and a reason to call you.

    “Hurricane season starts June 1. Here’s a five-minute policy check.”

  • Your story

    Why you became an agent, who you serve and what you stand for. Trust starts with knowing who you are.

    “Why I became an insurance agent.”

  • Your community

    Local events, sponsorships and the people you serve, so your page feels like a neighbor, not an ad.

    “Proud to sponsor our youth soccer league this season.”

  • Your face, every week

    Branded images made from your own headshots. People trust an agent they can picture.

Your approval

Nothing posts until you say so.

Insurance advertising has rules, and many agencies and carriers have their own on top. LiveLoop is built around your approval: each week you review the drafted posts, then approve the week, edit a post or pass on it.

  • Review before it posts

    See every drafted post for the week before anything goes out. If your agency reviews social posts, run your week by them, then approve.

  • Set what you can’t say

    Add banned words and topics to your Brand Kit, like “lowest rates” or a carrier you can’t name, and LiveLoop writes around them.

  • A heads-up before it’s due

    If a post still needs your OK, you get a reminder before it’s due. Every caption also gets an editor check before it reaches you.

LiveLoop doesn’t replace your compliance review. You, your agency and your carrier decide what’s right to post.

Insurance agent social media: questions, answered.

What should insurance agents post on social media?

Posts that build trust before someone needs a quote: plain-language coverage explainers, life moments like a new home or a teen driver, seasonal reminders, your story and your community. LiveLoop plans that mix every week from your Signature DNA.

Can I review posts for compliance before they go out?

Yes. Nothing posts until you approve it. Each week you review the drafted posts, then approve the week, edit a post or pass on it, so you or your compliance team can check every post first. LiveLoop doesn’t replace that review. It gives you the time to do it.

Can I keep certain words or topics out of my posts?

Yes. Add them to your Brand Kit as banned words or messaging guidelines, such as rate promises, carrier names or products you don’t offer, and LiveLoop writes around them. Every caption also gets an editor check before it reaches you.

I’m a captive agent. Can I use LiveLoop?

Many carriers have their own social media rules, and some require approved content or tools. Check your agent agreement or ask your carrier first. If you’re cleared to run your own page, LiveLoop works the same way for you: nothing posts until you approve it.

Is LiveLoop a person or software?

LiveLoop is a service from Signature Branding & Marketing that runs on AI built around your Signature DNA: your story, your voice and your topics. Every caption gets an editor check, and nothing posts until you approve it. Curious how the AI side works? Read about our AI social media manager.

Will the posts sound like me?

That’s what LiveLoop is built for. Your Signature DNA interview captures why you became an agent, who you serve and how you talk, and LiveLoop writes from that. Your images come from your own headshots, not stock.

Which platforms does LiveLoop post to?

Your Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn. The LiveLoop plans page lists every account you can connect.

Do I need professional headshots first?

It helps. People want to see the agent they’re trusting with their coverage. A Signature headshot session gives LiveLoop real photos of you to work from. If you already have photos you like, AI Branding turns them into branded images.
