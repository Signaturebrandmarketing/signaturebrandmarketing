What does a social media manager do?

Most small business owners know they should post more. The hard part is doing it every week while running the business. That’s the job a social media manager takes off your plate.

Here’s what the job involves, and how LiveLoop handles each part.

  • Plans your content

    A good manager knows what you should talk about. LiveLoop plans your week from your Signature DNA, the story, voice and topics captured in your Brand Kit.

  • Writes your posts

    Captions that sound like you, not like a template. LiveLoop writes every post in your voice, and every caption gets an editor check before it reaches you.

  • Creates the visuals

    Your posts use branded images made from your own headshots. Your face, your look, no stock photos.

  • Posts on schedule

    Once you approve your week, posts go out to your Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn on schedule. Each month also becomes your Monthly Issue, a digital magazine of your month.

  • Keeps you in the loop

    Nothing posts without your OK. If something still needs your approval, you get a heads-up before it’s due. And LiveLoop learns from the posts you pick and how they perform.

Hiring a social media manager: questions, answered.

What does a social media manager do for a small business?

A social media manager plans your content, writes the captions, creates the images, schedules the posts and keeps an eye on what’s working. Signature LiveLoop does each of those every week. Your part is approving your week.

How much does it cost to hire a social media manager for a small business?

In the US, a freelancer typically runs about $300 to $1,500 a month, an agency about $1,000 to $3,000 a month for a small business, and an in-house hire about $50,000 to $87,000 a year. LiveLoop’s plans and prices are on the LiveLoop plans page.

Should I hire a freelancer, an agency or use LiveLoop?

A freelancer or an agency gives you a person to brief, check in with and approve. A hire adds recruiting, payroll and managing. LiveLoop does the weekly work for you from your Signature DNA, and your part is approving your week. If you want posts that sound like you every week without managing anyone, LiveLoop is built for that.

Is LiveLoop a person or software?

LiveLoop is a service from Signature Branding & Marketing that runs on AI built around your Signature DNA: your story, your voice and your topics. Every caption gets an editor check, and nothing posts until you approve it. Curious how the AI side works? Read about our AI social media manager.

Will the posts sound like me?

That’s what LiveLoop is built for. It learns your story, your voice and your topics before it writes, and it uses branded images made from your own headshots instead of stock.

Do I approve posts before they go out?

Always. Each week you see your drafted posts, then approve the whole week in one tap, edit a post, or pass on it. If something still needs your OK, you get a heads-up before it’s due.

Which platforms does LiveLoop post to?

Your Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn. The LiveLoop plans page lists every account you can connect.

Do I need professional photos first?

It helps. A Signature headshot session gives LiveLoop real photos of you to work from. If you already have photos you like, AI Branding turns them into branded images.
